Saki Danmaku - How to Play

1. Unzip the folder first.
2. Open index.html in Google Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
3. Click through Saki's opening dialogue.
4. Use WASD or arrow keys to move.
5. Press Space or click/tap to fire extra bullets.
6. Defeat hostile comments, collect coins, level up, and try to reach 10,000 points.

Important:
Keep the assets folder next to index.html. The game needs it for Saki's image and the background music.
